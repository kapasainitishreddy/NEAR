Here's how Receipts works.
Step one. Pick what you need to say, and generate a calm script in three tones.
Edit it freely, then copy or read it aloud.
Step two. When you make a real decision, record why — the reasons, the trade-offs,
and a clarity score, all for future you.
Step three. Over time, Receipts shows how you actually decide,
with a calibration score and your personal patterns.
Everything lives in your library, search and filter it anytime.
Make it yours with four calm themes.
Private, local-first, and yours alone.
