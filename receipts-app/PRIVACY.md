# Privacy Policy — Receipts

_Last updated: 2026-06-12_

Receipts is a **local-first** app. Your privacy is the whole point.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Receipts has no backend, no account system, and no servers that
receive your content.

- Your scripts, decision receipts, notes, reviews, and personal rules are stored
  **only on your device** (in the app's local storage / IndexedDB).
- We do **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.
- We use **no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no cookies** for
  tracking.

## Network use

The app functions offline. The only optional network request is loading a web
font for styling; this involves no personal data. Native builds bundle assets
locally.

## Your data, your control

- **Export:** you can export your data to a JSON file that you choose to save or
  share. Nothing is uploaded unless *you* export and share it yourself.
- **Import:** you can restore from a JSON file you previously exported.
- **Delete:** you can clear all entries or reset the app at any time from
  Settings. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

## Children's privacy

Receipts does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Third parties

No data is shared with third parties because no data leaves your device.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here.
