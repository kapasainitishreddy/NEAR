Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Receipts is a local-first app — privacy is the whole point. Everything you write stays on your device by default. A few optional features only send data when you choose to use them, and those are spelled out plainly below.

The short version

By default, Receipts collects nothing and uploads nothing. No account, no tracking, no ads, no analytics.

What we store

Your scripts, decision receipts, notes, reviews, outcomes, and personal rules are stored only on your device (local storage / IndexedDB). We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share this content. We use no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking cookies.

Optional features that use the network

These are off unless you turn them on or tap them:

  • AI Co-pilot (optional): if enabled by the app owner and you tap an AI action, the specific text you’re working on is sent to an AI provider (e.g. Anthropic) through a secure proxy to generate a suggestion. That text is processed to return a response and is not used to identify you.
  • Ask-for-advice polls (optional): if you choose to create a poll, the decision text and options you include are stored on our polling service so the people you share the link with can vote anonymously. Don’t include anything you wouldn’t want those recipients to read. Polls auto-expire.
  • Purchases (optional): if you buy Receipts Pro, the purchase is handled by the App Store / Google Play and RevenueCat, which process purchase and receipt data to manage your subscription. See their privacy policies.
  • Sharing: when you use “Share,” your device’s share sheet sends the content to whatever app you pick. Fonts: the web version may load a web font for styling (no personal data).

Voice features

Dictation and read-aloud are handled by your device/browser’s built-in speech engine. Receipts does not record or upload audio.

Your data, your control

  • Export your data to a JSON file you choose to save or share.
  • Import from a file you previously exported.
  • Delete everything or reset the app anytime in Settings. Uninstalling removes all local data.

Children’s privacy

Receipts does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
